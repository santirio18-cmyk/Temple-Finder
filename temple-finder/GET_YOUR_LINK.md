# 🔗 Get Your App Link

## 📍 How to Find Your Vercel App URL

### Step 1: Go to Vercel Dashboard
👉 **https://vercel.com/dashboard**

### Step 2: Find Your Project
- Look for project: **`Temple-Finder`** (or your project name)
- Click on it

### Step 3: Get Your URL
You'll see your deployment URL at the top:
```
✅ Production: https://temple-finder-xxx.vercel.app
```

**This is your public link!** 🔗

---

## 🚀 If Not Deployed Yet

### Complete Deployment First:

1. **Sign in to Vercel** (if not already)
2. **Import Project:**
   - Click "Add New Project"
   - Find: `santirio18-cmyk/Temple-Finder`
   - Click "Import"

3. **Configure:**
   - Root Directory: `temple-finder`
   - Click "Deploy"

4. **Wait 1-2 minutes**

5. **Get Your Link:**
   - After deployment completes
   - You'll see: `https://your-app-name.vercel.app`
   - This is your public link!

---

## 📱 Your App Link Format

Your link will look like:
```
https://temple-finder-xxx.vercel.app
```

Or if you set a custom domain:
```
https://temple-finder.com
```
